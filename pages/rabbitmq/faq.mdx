## Overview

### What is Cloud Essentials for RabbitMQ?

Cloud Essentials for RabbitMQ is a managed messaging service that allows you to deploy and manage RabbitMQ instances on Scaleway infrastructure.

## Getting started

### How do I connect to my RabbitMQ instance?

To use your RabbitMQ instance, you need to enter your credentials and endpoint URL in the Scaleway console in the `Network` section of your RabbitMQ Dashboard.

### Can I access the RabbitMQ Management Plugin?

Yes, every Cloud Essentials for RabbitMQ instance comes with the Management Plugin pre-installed and enabled. You can access the web-based interface directly from the `Management UI` button in the Scaleway console. This interface allows you to monitor your queues, exchanges, and connections, as well as manage users and virtual hosts (vhosts) visually.

## Quotas and limitations

### What limitations apply during the Public Beta?

The following limitations apply to Cloud Essentials for RabbitMQ in its current Public Beta phase:
- Only RabbitMQ 4.2 version is supported
- Very large message backlogs may impact performance
- Advanced RabbitMQ features like Dead Letter Exchange (DLX) are restricted to ensure the stability of the service
